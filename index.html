Productivity & focus

I have 30 tabs open and can't focus on anything.

The One Tab Rule

Close everything except the tab for your current task. Need a reference later? Bookmark it instead of leaving it open.

My to-do list has 40 items and I feel paralyzed.

The Rule of 3

Every morning, pick only three tasks that matter today. Everything else waits its turn.

I keep starting tasks but never finishing them.

The 2-Minute Close

Before switching tasks, spend two minutes writing exactly where you left off. Future-you will thank present-you.

Money & finance

Budgeting feels overwhelming and I give up after a week.

The One Number Budget

Track just one number daily: money spent. No categories, no spreadsheets — awareness alone changes behavior.

I never know if I can afford something.

The 24-Hour Rule

For any non-essential purchase over a set amount, wait 24 hours. If you still want it, buy it.

Saving money feels impossible with irregular income.

Percentage-Based Saving

Save a fixed percentage of every payment you receive, not a fixed dollar amount. It scales automatically with you.

Home & life admin

My house is cluttered and cleaning feels like a huge project.

The 10-Minute Reset

Set a timer for ten minutes each evening and put things back where they belong. No deep cleaning required.

I forget to do routine maintenance — filters, renewals, and the like.

The Monthly Home Check

One recurring calendar reminder on the 1st of each month — a single prompt to review everything at once.

Meal planning takes too much mental energy.

The 5 Meal Rotation

Pick five meals you enjoy and rotate them weekly. Decision fatigue removed entirely.

Communication & relationships

Difficult conversations always spiral out of control.

Name It First

Open with "I want to talk about this because I care about it working." Naming intent lowers defensiveness.

I say yes to everything and end up overcommitted.

The Default Delay

Default reply: "Let me check and get back to you." It removes the pressure to decide on the spot.

Group plans never get finalized.

One Proposal, Not a Poll

One person proposes a specific time and place. Everyone else only needs to say yes or no.

Tech & digital life

I have too many passwords to remember safely.

One Vault, One Master

A password manager plus one strong master password solves security and memory at the same time.

My phone feels addictive and I lose hours to it.

Grayscale Mode

Turn the screen to grayscale. Removing color removes a huge share of the dopamine pull.

I get overwhelmed by notifications.

Calls and Texts Only

Turn off every notification except calls and texts from real people. Check apps on your schedule, not theirs.

Health & wellbeing

I know I should exercise but never "find the time."

The 2-Minute Start

Commit only to putting on your shoes and stepping outside. Starting is the only hard part.

I don't drink enough water during the day.

The One Bottle Method

Fill one large bottle each morning — that's your entire day's goal, visible and trackable at a glance.

Stress builds up and I don't notice until I'm overwhelmed.

The Daily Number

A quick 1–10 stress check-in, same time every day. Naming the number early catches problems before they compound.

Environment & sustainability

Living sustainably feels like it requires a total lifestyle overhaul.

One Swap a Month

Reusable bags this month, a reusable bottle next. Small compounding changes beat one overwhelming one.

Food waste keeps piling up.

The Eat Me First Bin

A visible bin in the fridge reserved only for items nearing expiration.

Learning & growth

I want to learn a new skill but never have a "big enough" block of time.

The 15-Minute Rule

Commit to only fifteen minutes a day. Consistency beats intensity.

I read and watch a lot but retain very little.

The One Sentence Summary

After anything you learn, write one sentence on what it means for you.
